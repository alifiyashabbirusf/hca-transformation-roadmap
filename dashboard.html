Metric definitions and design annotations

The dashboard contains eight metrics: five leading indicators, including organizational change health through active adoption, and three lagging time-saving outcomes. Adoption must be shown separately for I4, I5 and I6; an average must never hide a failing tool or site. Run displays illustrative prerequisites at Q6, not completed rollout achievements. Adoption values show the lowest site rate for each tool: I4 84%, I5 81% and I6 76%; the card uses the minimum of these, avoiding averages that conceal a struggling site. All figures are fictional, including the five proposed wave sites.

  • Chart choice: current-versus-target cards prioritize the gate decision. The labeled example values demonstrate one Q6 review. No trend arrows are drawn from a single illustrative period; add trends only after comparable observations are available.
  • Alert application: the 90% warning band is a proposed design convention, not HCA policy. For mandatory readiness and safety gates, any verified failure blocks release even if the completion percentage is high. Missing evidence never counts as a passed gate.
  • Review procedure: collect readiness sign-offs and pilot baseline/results, verify quality and safety audits, inspect adoption by tool and site, then record D6, D1 and D7 decisions with the named Accountable roles. D7 is conditional until a below-target site is documented.
